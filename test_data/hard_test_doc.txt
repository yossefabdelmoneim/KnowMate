
KnowMate — Internal Training & Policy Document

1. RETURN POLICY
Customers may return any product within 45 days of purchase.
Items must be in original packaging. Refunds are issued within
3 business days after inspection.

2. HR POLICY
All employees are entitled to 25 paid vacation days per year.
Parental leave: 16 weeks fully paid. Sick leave: 10 days per year.
Remote work: up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Health insurance: fully covered including dental and vision.

3. MARKETING GUIDELINES
Brand voice: professional, confident, approachable.
Tagline: "Know your data. Know your world."
Target audience: enterprise decision-makers (CTOs, VPs of Eng).
Key differentiators: on-premise deployment, no data leaks,
local LLM inference, RAG-powered accuracy.
Q4 campaign focus: security-first AI for regulated industries.
